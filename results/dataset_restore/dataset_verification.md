## 2. Split methodology (documented + verified live)

`ml/prepare_dataset.py` (git commit `d08e860684379a6586bd0bc6fdcabcff0968c073` "T2.1: dataset preparation",
sha256 `71775b4dd1067589e2b210daaaecd808dd1a8dbe8927c881ca71a6538e126843`; byte-identical to the copy that
generated the original manifest) — unedited during restoration:

- **Split seed:** `SPLIT_SEED = 42` (fixed, in-code).
- **TrashCan rule:** official train+val combined (7,212 images), grouped by video ID
  (regex `^(vid_\d+)_frame\d+\.jpg$`; 312 unique videos), videos sorted then `random.Random(42).shuffle`,
  15 % of videos → test (46), remainder 90/10 → train (240) / val (26). Official split had 113/115 val
  videos shared with train (98.3 % leakage) — the re-split removes it.
- **DUO rule:** official test split kept intact as test (1,111 images); official train (6,671) sorted,
  `random.Random(42).shuffle`, 10 % → val (667), remainder → train (6,004).
- **Leakage assertions in-script:** no TrashCan video in two splits; no image path in two splits.
