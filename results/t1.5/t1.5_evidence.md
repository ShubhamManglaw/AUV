## 5. Acceptance interpretation (human-approved amendment, 2026-09-29)

The original criterion ("GT track ≤ 1.5 m from planned legs away from turns; demo mission ≈ 4 min") mixed
waypoint-controller tracking with accumulated navigation error. With normal T1.3 dead reckoning the GT
intended-leg deviation reaches 5.3310 m max (62.52 % > 1.5 m) — accumulated navigation error over the full
mission, not waypoint pursuit. Per human ruling: T1.5 controller acceptance is validated against the
labelled perfect-navigation baseline (≤ 1.5 m, passed); normal T1.3 drift stays enabled for
production/end-to-end M1 evaluation with GT deviation reported separately; the ≈ 4 min expectation is
replaced by measured duration. Original wording preserved in `docs/implementation_plan.md` §15.2 and
`docs/CHANGELOG.md`.
